# Affiliate Marketer Workflow: From Login to Profitable Content

## Complete Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### **Step 1: Login & Dashboard Overview**
**What you see:** Your personalized dashboard showing:
- Quick stats (campaigns, content, performance)
- Upgrade prompt (if on Standard tier - "Upgrade to Pro" button in header)
- Quick action buttons for common tasks
- Recent campaigns and content

**What you do:** Explore the interface and familiarize yourself with the layout

---

### **Step 2: Browse the Product Library**
**What you see:** Catalog of products from product developers, each with:
- Product thumbnail and name
- Commission rate (e.g., "50%", "$37/sale")
- Affiliate network (e.g., WarriorForum, JVZoo)
- Times used by affiliates
- Compliance status

**What you do:**
1. Browse products by category or search
2. **Filter by:** Commission type (recurring vs one-time), network, or popularity
3. **Sort by:** Recent additions, commission rate, or most popular
4. Click on any product to view full details

**💡 Pro Tip:** Products with recurring commissions are goldmines - they pay you monthly for referred customers!

---

### **Step 3: Create Your First Campaign**
**What you see:** Create Campaign button on any product detail page

**What you do:**
1. Click "Create Campaign" on a product you want to promote
2. **Fill in your unique affiliate link** (you get this from the affiliate network)
3. Add relevant keywords for your target audience
4. Name your campaign (use descriptive names like "Supplement Review - Molityn")
5. Click "Create Campaign"

**Result:** Campaign is created and automatically linked to the product's intelligence data

---

### **Step 4: Generate Campaign Intelligence** (Optional but Recommended)
**What you see:** "Compile Intelligence" option in your campaign

**What you do:**
1. Go to Intelligence page
2. Select your new campaign from dropdown
3. Click "Compile Intelligence"
4. Wait 30-60 seconds while AI analyzes the product

**What you get:**
- ✅ Target audience demographics and pain points
- ✅ Product benefits and key selling points
- ✅ Competitor analysis
- ✅ Marketing hooks and angles
- ✅ Objection handling strategies

**Why this matters:** Campaigns with intelligence generate 3x better content because AI knows exactly who to target and how to position the product!

---

### **Step 5: Create High-Converting Content**
**What you see:** Content Generation Hub with two options:
- **Text Content:** Articles, emails, videos, social posts, landing pages, ad copy
- **Image Content:** Hero images, social graphics, thumbnails

**What you do for Text Content:**
1. Go to Content → Text Content
2. Select your campaign
3. Choose content type (e.g., "Review Article" or "Email Sequence")
4. Set parameters:
   - **Marketing Angle:** Problem/Solution, Transformation, Authority, etc.
   - **Tone:** Professional, Casual, Friendly, etc.
   - **Length:** Short, Medium, Long
5. Click "Generate"

**What you get:**
- ✅ AI-generated content based on campaign intelligence
- ✅ Automatic FTC compliance checking
- ✅ Compliance score (Green = compliant, Red = needs fixing)
- ✅ "Fix Compliance" button to auto-add required disclosures

**💡 Pro Tip:** Generate 3-5 variations and A/B test to see what converts best!

---

### **Step 6: Review & Refine Content**
**What you see:** Generated content with:
- Full text content
- Compliance status
- Action buttons: Edit, Regenerate, Create Variations, Download, Copy

**What you do:**
1. Review the content for accuracy
2. If compliance issues flagged, click "Fix Compliance"
3. Edit any specific details you want to change
4. Create variations for A/B testing
5. Download or copy to your marketing channels

**Result:** Professional, compliant, high-converting marketing content ready to publish

---

### **Step 7: Build Your Content Library**
**What you see:** Content Library showing all your generated content across campaigns

**What you do:**
1. Browse "All Content" or filter by Text/Images
2. Search for specific content
3. Download content files
4. Create variations of top performers

**Why this matters:** Build a swipe file of your best content for future campaigns. Refine high-performers instead of starting from scratch!

---

### **Step 8: Track Your Performance**
**What you see:** Analytics dashboard showing:
- Total clicks from your content
- Conversion tracking
- Revenue earned
- Top-performing campaigns
- Best content types

**What you do:**
1. Check Analytics weekly
2. Identify top-performing content
3. Double down on winning strategies
4. Fix or retire underperforming content

**💡 Pro Tip:** Focus 80% of your effort on content types and angles that are driving actual sales!
