A: Traditional networks focus on transactions. Blitz focuses on relationships. We help you build long-term partnerships with both affiliates and developers, plus connect with peers in your industry.

**Q: Can I really message developers directly?**
A: Yes! Once connected, you can message directly. No more generic support emails or waiting for responses through middlemen.

**Q: What if I'm new to affiliate marketing?**
A: Perfect! Our community is designed to help you learn. Connect with experienced affiliates, get mentorship, and learn from real success stories.

**Q: Do you take commissions on my sales?**
A: No. We never take a cut of your affiliate commissions or product sales. Your success is your success.

**Q: Is this only for big companies?**
A: Not at all! We have successful affiliates and developers at every stage, from beginners launching their first product to seasoned pros scaling their operations.
